Read privacy notes
Privacy in plain English

Your searches, your choice.

MiniFind does not ask you to create an account. When you use Web, Images or Videos, your search terms are sent to the selected external search provider. Quick answers uses Wikipedia's public search API. Saved links are kept in local browser storage on your device. MiniFind does not currently collect email addresses.
